Study case: 2028 TC2 Phase II (Summer Peak) — the current one, harvested by us, 1,826 of 1,826 AEP buses in BOTH directions. Headroom is the tightest facility the load stresses (|dfax|≥5%) that is not already over its rating; pre-existing overloads are still counted and flagged, they are only excluded from the choice of what binds. ⚠ This is headroom at a 100 MW probe, not the bus maximum — measured, our figure does not vary with the requested MW, so closing the gap to a vendor-style maximum needs a different derivation, not a bigger scrape. ⚠ Only 227 of the 1,826 carry a location we trust, so most cannot be placed on a map or matched to a parcel yet.
