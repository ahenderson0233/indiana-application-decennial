1 What are you building?
A data centre builds over or removes what is there, so its buildable area is the whole parcel. A battery fits around existing buildings, so it uses open ground (parcel − building footprints).

2 Land & size
“Undeveloped” is about land; “vacant” is about a building. Undeveloped land simply has no structure and is not a sign of distress — it is the cleanest BESS land we hold, with nothing to demolish and no tenants to relieve. A vacant or abandoned building is an owner-motivation signal — section 4.

Loads on first use — 3.7 MB. Not every screener site carries a location; the count is stated once it loads.
Rail and highway access decide how construction loads and transformers reach the site. Federal surplus is land a government body has already declared it does not need — a rare case where the seller is publicly committed to disposing.
3 Power & grid
These measure distance to a wire, which is not the same as capacity to spare. A parcel beside a full substation is worse than one three miles from a connection point with headroom. For capacity, use the Site screener.
Shown on the map
Voltage class is the fastest read on the map — a 765 kV backbone and a sub-100 kV lateral look the same on a plain map and are not remotely the same siting fact. Unknown voltage has its own colour and is never drawn as low voltage. Gas matters if you intend on-site generation; a 300 MW plant needs roughly 50 MMcf/d. Large emitters signal neighbours who already hold air permits, so the permitting path is precedented rather than novel.
4 Owner motivation
Public records — unpaid taxes, a foreclosure, a demolition order — suggesting an owner may transact. Non-residential only, and only trouble serious enough to plausibly move someone to sell.
Undated is not old. 90% of our largest signal (unpaid taxes) carries no date, so a bare “last 90 days” cut would delete nine tenths of it for a reason about our collection, not about the property. They are kept by default; untick to exclude them.
5 Environmental constraints
The first three are hard constraints that can stop a project. The last is an upside — areas where a coal plant or mine closed carry a larger federal tax credit.
Shown on the map
A watershed is the unit water is actually allocated and argued over in — two parcels a mile apart in different subbasins can face different objections and different permitting bodies. Click one to see the rivers, reservoirs and lakes it holds. Power generation is already Indiana’s largest water user (3,822 of 7,177 Mgal/d) and 99.5% of that is surface water, so a new thermal load competes for exactly what these layers show.
6 Community & local rules
County-level, not parcel-level. Opposition intensity partly tracks news volume, so large metros read higher for reasons that are not posture. Absence of a recorded rule is not evidence there is no rule — Indiana's data-centre regulation is mostly county moratoria that no code library carries.

Existing data centres are both competition for the same power and evidence a county has already accepted one. ⚠ An approval never cancels a ban: where a county has both, the restriction still governs.
Rank the results your weights
Weights are yours to set — these are defaults, not an answer. A part with no data for a site is left out of that site's average, never scored zero, so a site is never punished for our coverage gap.
