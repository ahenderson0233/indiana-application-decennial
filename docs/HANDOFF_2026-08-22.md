## ⛔ 1. THE TWO OPERATOR RULINGS THAT SHAPED EVERYTHING

**① *"Even if a source scrapes everything but one column, we still want to rescrape it for
everything because that one field may contain something materially important."*** That converts
G152 from a ranking exercise into an arithmetic one. Every source is clipped at FULL WIDTH;
ranking governs only the order. `build_si_upstream_wide.py` uses `SELECT *` and asserts the column
count equals the parent's — naming columns is how a clip narrows.

**② *"The only calculation that matters is the nearest transmission line and the two endpoints of
that transmission line ending at the two bus endpoints… the minimum value of the two buses…
When only one end resolves, JUST take that one bus value… and to ensure this is accurate, match
the buses to the substations, which are linked to the transmission lines."*** Four messages, and
together they turned G131 from a correctness check into a **66% coverage gain**.
⚠ Read ① and ② together: **the substation is a BRIDGE, never a validity test.** The operator said
in the same exchange that *"the bus doesn't have to be near the substation asset"* — we do not
reject a bus for being far from a station; we use the station to FIND one.
