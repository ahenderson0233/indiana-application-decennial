published row for bus 243209 (2844 MVA, 2550 MW, −0.0745 → **3,946 vs their 3,943.59**). We do not

- their binder is **present and passes our filters** on 134 buses → genuine ranking work
- their binder is **absent from our harvest entirely** on **146 of 298**

⭐ **Placement: the method is proven, the gazetteer is the ceiling.** Bus label → substation name
(stripping the AEP area prefix, `05EUGENE` → `EUGENE`) reproduces the vendor's coordinates at a

the gazetteer is the unlock, not a better matcher.**

Michigan. `in_substations` is Indiana-only. **283, not 1,826, is the number that matters.**
