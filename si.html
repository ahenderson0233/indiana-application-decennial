So the raw file is narrowed three times: to structure fires, to non-residential property, and to material loss. ⚠ Does an environmental violation really mean someone will sell?

First, the volume was never real. The corpus is often quoted at 58,003 rows, but 50,334 of those (86.8%) carry no_distress_marker and were never admitted. What is left is small enough to argue about one band at a time — which is the only honest way to answer the question.
bandparcelsthe mechanism to a SALEverdict
Facility inactive
stopped reporting to ECHO
 184 The strongest of the four and not really a “violation” at all: a facility that has stopped reporting has usually stopped operating. That is a vacated industrial site, which is the thing a siter is looking for. KEPT
Significant violation
EPA SNC / HPV designation
 251 EPA’s own formal finding of serious non-compliance. It triggers a consent decree or major capital remediation, and a marginal facility often divests rather than invests. KEPT
Penalised
monetary penalty assessed
 251 A dated, quantified cost booked against this specific site. Weaker than the two above, but it is a real financial event on the asset rather than a paperwork finding. KEPT, banded lower
Plain violation
routine non-compliance finding
 676 ⛔ No defensible mechanism. Most violations are corrected and the owner carries on. This is the environmental twin of the standing ruling that maintenance activity does not prove a willing seller. DEMOTED 2026-08-19b
⚠ The demotion cost 482 admitted parcels — the flagged total moved from 24,277 to 23,795. The rows were not deleted: they carry admit_status='excluded_low_severity' and remain queryable, so the decision can be reversed with one predicate if you disagree with it.

Signal inventory — the upstream corpus table, not the whole program

Read this as one upstream table, not as leads and not as the full signal set. It holds … observations across … signals, before any ruling is applied. Two cautions, because this table has read as more complete than it is:
1. It is not the whole program. … signals appear in the coverage table further down, because owner-grain signals, absentee ownership and environmental enforcement are held in their own tables and never flowed through this one. A signal missing here is not a signal we lack.
2. Size here does not mean value. The largest row is undeveloped land, which the operator ruled is not a signal at all, and … of these are recorded against a company, a court case or a rail corridor and can never reach a parcel. Only … parcels survive residential exclusion and the severity test — those are the leads, and they are at the top of this page.
