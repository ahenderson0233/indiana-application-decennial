They are ranked by how many different kinds of distress stack up on one property, because a single signal can be noise while a demolition order sitting on top of an unsafe building on top of repeated violations is a story you can open a conversation with. Read the why column, then take the parcel to the map console for its grid and land position.

Structure fires severe enough to move an owner to sell

A signal only counts when it plausibly produces seller intent. A contained kitchen fire does not, and a house is not a hyperscale site. So the raw file is narrowed three times: to structure fires, to non-residential property, and to material loss. Each step is shown, so the discard is auditable rather than a number appearing from nowhere.
